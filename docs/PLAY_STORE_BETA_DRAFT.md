# RouteRevive — Google Play Listing / Beta Draft

**Working title:** RouteRevive: Service Business Planner

**Short description (under 80 characters):**
Manage service jobs, routes, estimates, invoices and repeat customers.

**Long description (draft, verify the final build):**

Keep your service business organized from the first customer inquiry to a finished job.

RouteRevive is a local-first Android planner for pressure-washing contractors and other appointment-based service providers. Record customer information, schedule visits, view mapped work locations, and manage a daily route from one app.

Create service estimates and invoices as shareable PDFs. Attach before-and-after photos to jobs and manually record deposits or payments. Use clear financial summaries to review issued invoices, balances, and recorded income.

RouteRevive includes an owner-managed booking request inbox and a shareable HTML request form. Customer inquiries do not automatically become confirmed appointments. You review incoming requests and approve the time.

For repeat customers, build neighborhood campaigns by ZIP code with documented SMS marketing permission, individual message approval, and manual sending through your phone's SMS app. Opted-out customers are suppressed.

Customer/job records are stored in the app's private local storage. Export encrypted full backups with photos when you choose.

**Important:** This version does not offer online accounts, automatic messaging, live hosted booking pages, real-time traffic optimization, payment processing, or multi-device cloud sync.
